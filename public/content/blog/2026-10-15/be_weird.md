Yes, it's been a couple of months.  Job searching doesn't help side projects, although that *might* be over?  It's certainly in a bit of an odd state right now.

But I was filling in a cover letter as a safety net, and thought about the LLM tools that especially plague the world of recruitment at the moment.  Fake candidates, generated CVs, the whole hog.

And I decided.. why not get weird with it?  Not in a surreal or absurd way, just a little less filtered.

Because LLMs will inevitably regress everything towards a bland mean: they can copy *styles*, but make yours spiky enough and they'll choke on it.  Typos are optional (I *am* dyslexic after all), but whenever you feel yourself turning to a overly formal or corporate word choice, **stop**.

Think about how you actually sound and want to sound.  Loosen that tie.  Put on comfy socks.  And just write without overly considering what you *should* sound like.

It's kind of fun.

Not that I got the job, but this was an unprompted submission to a job directly and they rarely bear fruit.  But in a world where a large volume of blandly regurgitated text is produced constantly, you don't want to look like just another part of it.
